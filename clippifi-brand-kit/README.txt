Clippifi brand kit


WHAT'S INSIDE

Logo/              Full logo in black, white, gradient and two-colour
                   (gradient symbol with black or white type). SVG and PNG.
Symbol/            The symbol on its own in black, white and gradient.
                   SVG and PNG.
Wordmark/          The wordmark on its own in black, white and gradient.
                   SVG and PNG.
App Icons/         Rounded app icons, 1080 x 1080 PNG. The yellow one is the
                   main mark: charcoal symbol on solid yellow. Also includes
                   yellow on black and other colourways, plus flat black and
                   white mono versions.
Profile Pictures/  Square profile pictures, 1080 x 1080 PNG. Use
                   clippifi-pfp-yellow.png by default.
Social/            X header image, 1500 x 500, PNG and JPG.
Favicons/          The main mark as 16, 32 and 512 px PNGs, a 180 px Apple
                   touch icon and a multi-size favicon.ico.
Fonts/             Geist typeface (Geist.zip, SIL Open Font License).

Use the SVG files where you can. The PNGs are for places that don't accept SVG.

COLOURS

Yellow     #FFE873   rgb(255, 232, 115)   main brand colour
White      #FFFFFF   rgb(255, 255, 255)
Charcoal   #252525   rgb(37, 37, 37)
Gradient   #FFE873 (top) -> #FFF0A3 (bottom)

Shades: Yellow and Charcoal at 70%, 50%, 30% and 10% opacity.

Yellow is light, so it is a background colour: put charcoal on yellow, never
yellow text or a yellow logo on white. On white or light backgrounds use the
black logo; use the yellow (gradient) logo on charcoal or black.

USING THE LOGO

- Keep the logo at least 32 px tall on screen.
- Always scale it proportionally. Don't stretch or squash it.
- Use the colour versions exactly as supplied. Don't recolour them.
- Write the name: Clippifi.
